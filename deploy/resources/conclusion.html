The daemon is installed and running, but it cannot reach your bridge until you pair with it. Open Terminal and run:

hue-scene-keeper auth

Press the round link button on the bridge when prompted. The agent retries every 30 seconds, so it picks up the new credentials on its own — there is nothing to restart.

Check what it sees with hue-scene-keeper list, and follow it with tail -f ~/Library/Logs/hue-scene-keeper.log.
